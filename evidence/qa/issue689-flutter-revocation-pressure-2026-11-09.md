# Issue 689: Flutter revocation under backlog

- Two focused tests failed before the change: a `voice.lease_revoked` event remained behind a blocked delivery and was discarded when a full backlog overflowed. Flutter now starts handling that urgent event immediately, deduplicates it, and still advances its durable cursor in queue order only after the event and earlier work complete. Overflow or an urgent-handler failure clears the cursor and requests recovery; a late completion from the previous generation cannot acknowledge work. The prior 131-line delivery implementation was split into a 95-line entry and a 60-line private pump part.
